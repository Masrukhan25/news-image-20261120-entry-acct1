# Friday 20 November 2026 — the whole day's world news in one picture

One page, twenty stories, all from Friday 20 November 2026. Up top:
Russian missiles hitting three bridges in Kyiv, Cyclone Akvan coming
ashore on India's east coast, and the AI sell-off that sank Asia's
markets. Below them, ten more stories in a grid — the UK–EU reset deal,
COP31's last scheduled day, a missile in Poland, Iraq's army moving
into a militia stronghold, Italy's general strike, Japan's inflation,
Seoul's impeachment move, Kenya's first oil well, Phillips' 118 in
Wellington, and the UK's cyber chief — plus seven briefs and a world
map with twenty numbered pins, one per story.

Nothing on the page is from another day. Every story happened, or was
first reported, on Friday 20 November 2026; where a count comes from
officials, the poster says so. The red DATE CHECK box at the bottom
names the big Thursday stories that were left out on purpose (the
World Court's Myanmar ruling, the three-leader declaration in
Shenzhen, Tonga's drought emergency, the migrant boat lost off
Senegal) and the two things that had not happened yet (BepiColombo
entering orbit round Mercury, a final COP31 text).

The image is `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG). `main.py` — Python 3.13,
standard library only — copies it to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`), checking first that it really is the 1024×1024
PNG this entry ships, and that the copy is byte-identical.
